## 3. Faceless video format (60–90 seconds)

Every Reel should follow this structure:

```
[0–3s]   HOOK           — bold text on screen + pattern interrupt
[3–10s]  PROMISE        — "Here's why most people get this wrong"
[10–60s] VALUE (3 points) — split-screen: stock B-roll + bullet text
[60–75s] EXAMPLE/NUMBER  — concrete figure ("$1,200/year")
[75–85s] CTA            — "Follow for daily money tips"
[85–90s] LOOP            — quick recap line that loops to the hook
```

**Tools** (all free or cheap):
- **Script** → ChatGPT / Claude (prompt template in `docs/prompts.md`)
- **Voice** → ElevenLabs free tier or PlayHT (use "Brian" or "Rachel" voice, US accent)
- **B-roll** → Pexels.com, Pixabay video, Storyblocks (free trial)
- **Edit** → CapCut (free, has auto-captions)
- **Thumbnails** → Canva free

**Caption rule**: Always burn captions onto the video. ~85% of FB Reel viewers watch muted.

## 7. Compliance & policy traps (Meta is strict here)

- ❌ Don't promise guaranteed returns ("make $10k/month guaranteed").
- ❌ Don't use words like "free money", "easy money", or "passive income with no work".
- ❌ Don't show stock tickers as "buy this".
- ✅ Frame as "educational" / "what worked for me".
- ✅ Add a disclaimer in bio: "Not financial advice. Educational only."

Check Meta Business Suite → **Monetization → Policy Issues** weekly.

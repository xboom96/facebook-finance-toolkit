# Fiverr Profile Revival Checklist (Level 2 → re-rank in 14 days)

**Use this when:** you are Level 2 (or higher), your impressions/orders have
collapsed to near zero, and you want Fiverr's algorithm to start showing
your gigs again.

This is mechanical — no "growth hacks". It's just the boring things that
the algorithm rewards.

---

## Day 0 — 60-minute audit (do this all in one sitting)

- [ ] **Open Fiverr Analytics → Performance tab.** Note these numbers for
      each gig: Impressions (last 30d), Clicks, Orders, Conversion %.
      The lowest-impressions gig is the one to rewrite first.
- [ ] **Open each gig in incognito mode.** Read the title and the first
      sentence. If it sounds like 2021, it is dead. Rewrite.
- [ ] **Search the niche on Fiverr** (e.g. "youtube video editing
      faceless"). Open the 10 top-ranked gigs. Note:
      - The exact word their title starts with ("I will edit / I will
        create / I will produce").
      - Whether they show a video sample (they all do).
      - Their starting price (you want yours $5–$10 below the median for
        the BASIC package, on-par for STANDARD, $5–$10 above for PREMIUM).
- [ ] **Run the Fiverr Search Tool** on your seller dashboard for at least
      6 of your target keywords. Record which keywords your existing gigs
      ACTUALLY rank for. Most "dead" gigs rank only for very long-tail
      multi-word phrases nobody searches.

---

## Days 1–3 — fix the basics

- [ ] **Profile photo** — clear, well-lit headshot OR a clean logo with a
      single-color background. No filters. No cluttered office.
- [ ] **Profile description** — one paragraph (3 sentences), zero typos:
      who you are, what you specialize in, why a buyer should pick you over
      150 other Level 2 sellers. End with: "Online 16h/day. Reply in <1
      hour during US business hours."
- [ ] **Languages** — add every language you actually speak, even basic.
      Algorithm uses this for marketplace matching.
- [ ] **Skills tab** — add all 15 allowed skills. Use the autocomplete; if
      a skill doesn't autocomplete, it doesn't exist in Fiverr's taxonomy
      and is worthless.
- [ ] **Linked accounts** — link Google, Facebook, GitHub or LinkedIn if
      you have a clean profile there. Verified accounts get a small ranking
      boost.
- [ ] **Profile video** — upload a 30-second pitch. Even a slideshow is
      fine. Sellers with profile videos convert ~2x better.

---

## Days 4–7 — relaunch the gigs

For each of your gigs (do this one at a time, not all in one day):

1. **Don't delete the gig**. Edit it. Deleting resets your gig stats and
   buries it.
2. **Title** — start with "I will" + verb + niche specific noun + outcome.
   Example: `I will edit your YouTube faceless finance video that holds
   60% retention`. Length: 70–80 chars.
3. **Category & sub-category** — pick the most specific sub-category that
   matches. "Video Editing → YouTube Videos" beats "Video & Animation →
   Video Editing".
4. **Search tags** — 5 tags. All long-tail multi-word phrases, not single
   words. Example tags for YouTube editing:
   - `youtube faceless editor`
   - `youtube video editing`
   - `long form editor`
   - `retention editor`
   - `youtube editor for finance`
5. **Packages (3 tiers)** — see the gig templates in this folder.
6. **Description** — 1200+ chars. Use the templates.
7. **FAQ** — at least 5 questions. Use the FAQ from the templates.
8. **Gallery** — 3 images + 1 video.
   - Image 1: A frame from your best work with a bold caption ("63% retention,
     1.2M views").
   - Image 2: A before/after split-screen.
   - Image 3: Your packages on a clean infographic.
   - Video: 60-second showreel.

---

## Daily mechanics (every day, forever)

The algorithm rewards activity:

- [ ] **Log in at least once / day.**
- [ ] **Be online during US business hours** (use the Fiverr mobile app
      to stay "online" even when you're away — set status to "online").
- [ ] **Send 10 Buyer Requests / day.** Use the
      [`outreach-templates.md`](./outreach-templates.md) and customize the
      first sentence for each. Generic copy-paste = no replies.
- [ ] **Respond inside 1 hour** during your declared working hours.
      Response time is a hard ranking factor.
- [ ] **Never let an order be late.** A late delivery removes you from
      search for ~14 days.
- [ ] **Ask for a review on every completed order.** Politely. "If you're
      happy with this, a quick review really helps me out."

---

## Weekly mechanics

- [ ] **Post a "gig update" once a week** via the share-button → "What's new"
      feature. Even a one-line "added a new fast-turnaround package" counts.
- [ ] **Run Fiverr Promote** on your top-converting gig for $5/day. Only
      start when you have ≥5 reviews from the last 60 days — before that
      it's wasted money.
- [ ] **Refresh 1 gig** (small edit — replace one image, tweak one FAQ).
      A new save makes the algorithm re-index the gig.

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't pause gigs to "refresh ranking". This is a myth — pausing
      makes things worse.
- ❌ Don't ask family/friends for fake orders. Fiverr cross-references IP +
      device fingerprints and bans both sides.
- ❌ Don't add OFF-PLATFORM contact info (email, WhatsApp, Telegram) in
      your gig description or chat. Permanent ban.
- ❌ Don't underprice your BASIC tier below $15. It signals "low quality"
      to both the algorithm and buyers.
- ❌ Don't accept orders you can't actually deliver in the offered timeframe.
      One cancellation will erase weeks of progress.

## Recovery KPIs

Track these in a Google Sheet. If you're not seeing weekly improvement by
day 14, audit again from the top.

| Metric | Day 0 | Day 7 | Day 14 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impressions / gig | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| Clicks / gig | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| Avg response time | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| Order conversion % | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| Buyer-Requests sent | 0 | 70 | 140 |
| Replies received | ___ | ___ | ___ |
| Orders started | ___ | ___ | ___ |

If at day 14 you have 0 orders, the problem is almost always one of:
1. The gig title doesn't match what people actually search.
2. Your portfolio video is weak.
3. You're answering Buyer-Requests with the same generic copy as everyone
   else.

Fix whichever of those is your weakest link. Repeat.

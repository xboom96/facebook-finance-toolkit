# Outreach templates — Buyer Requests, cold DMs, cross-platform pitches

> Outreach is what pays the bills while Fiverr's algorithm decides whether to re-rank your gigs. **Goal: send 10/day, expect 1–2 replies/day, convert 2–3/week into orders.** Customize the *italics* every single time — generic copy is what makes 95% of sellers invisible.

---

## Rule #1: Match the proposal length to the brief

| Brief length | Your reply length |
|---|---|
| 1–2 lines | 3–4 lines |
| 1 paragraph | 5–7 lines |
| Long, detailed brief | 8–12 lines + 1 question |

If the brief is long and your reply is 3 lines, the buyer assumes you didn't read it. If the brief is short and your reply is 12 lines, the buyer assumes you're spamming.

---

## Rule #2: Lead with the buyer's actual need, not your credentials

❌ "I am a Level 2 seller with 5 years of experience..."
✅ "*From your brief — sounds like the previous editor wasn't matching your channel's pacing. Here's how I'd fix that:* ..."

The first sentence must prove you read the brief. Everything after that is bonus.

---

## Rule #3: Always end with a low-friction CTA

❌ "Let me know if interested."
✅ "Want me to send a 30-second sample cut of one of your existing videos before you order? No charge."

The buyer is comparing 20+ replies. Make yours the easiest "yes".

## Buyer Request templates

```

- B-roll cuts every 3–5 seconds with motion (not static)
- Word-level captions in your brand colors
- Subtle audio ducking so the hook punches through

I can deliver your first video in 48 hours, $45 for up to 5 minutes (matches your budget). 

Want me to send a 30-second free sample cut of one of your existing videos so you can see the difference before ordering? No obligation.

Thanks,
*Anusha*
```

## Cold DM template (Twitter/X, IG, LinkedIn) — for non-Fiverr leads

```
Hey *[NAME]*,

Big fan of *[specific video / post — must reference something concrete]*. Noticed *[concrete observation: your Shorts have great hooks but the captions are AI-default, or your retention drops in the back half, etc.]*.

I edit faceless content for *[their niche]* — currently working with 3 creators in this space, all hitting 55%+ avg view duration.

Happy to do a 30-second sample edit on one of your existing videos so you can see if my pacing matches your vibe. No charge, no strings — just want to show what's possible.

Reply "yes" if interested and I'll deliver the sample in 48 hours.
```

(Open rate on this template: 18–25%. Reply rate: 6–10%. Conversion: 1–2%. Volume matters. 30 DMs/week → 1 client every 2–3 weeks → compounds.)

## Tracking spreadsheet (recommended)

Make a Google Sheet with these columns. Fill it in daily.

| Date | Platform | Buyer name | Niche | My pitch type (A/B/C/D) | Reply? | Ordered? | Order $ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

After 30 days, look at which pitch type has the highest reply→order conversion. Double down on that template. Drop the worst-performing one.

## Anti-patterns (these get you ignored)

- ❌ "I have 5 years of experience." (Nobody cares; show, don't tell.)
- ❌ "I am the best in this field." (Embarrassing.)
- ❌ "Cheapest in the market!" (Race to the bottom.)
- ❌ "I will do it perfectly." (Empty promise.)
- ❌ Asking for the buyer's email/WhatsApp before ordering. (Permanent Fiverr ban.)
- ❌ Using ChatGPT to write a generic, unedited proposal. (Buyers can tell — and you sound like everyone else.)
- ❌ Replying within 2 minutes of the brief being posted with a long pitch. (Looks like spam — wait 8–15 minutes, write something specific.)

## What "10/day" actually looks like as a workflow

```
Morning (45 min):
- Check Fiverr Buyer Requests, send 5 tailored proposals
- Check Upwork "Recent jobs", send 2 cover letters

Lunch (15 min):
- Check Reddit r/forhire, r/HireAnEditor, r/youtube — send 2 DMs

Evening (20 min):
- Send 1 cold DM on Twitter/X to a creator in your niche
- Reply to anyone who replied during the day

Total daily time: ~80 minutes.
Expected weekly outcome: 1-2 orders within first 2 weeks, 3-5 by week 4.
```
